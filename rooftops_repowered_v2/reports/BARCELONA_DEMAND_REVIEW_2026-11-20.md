## Output and limits

`datasets/synthetic/barcelona_sector_reference/` contains reproducible 2023 normalized hourly sector shapes, generated by spreading each six-hour total evenly. They contain no building-specific energy magnitudes and cannot support claims of actual hourly behavior. Services does not identify office, school or hotel independently; residential is not a measurement of the selected housing. Source provenance, excluded records, and ineligibility flags are recorded alongside the output. Existing simulation surrogate and synthetic package remain unchanged.

## Proposed research-studio workflow

1. Maintain a shared evidence register with dataset owner, license, collection period, spatial and temporal resolution, units, limitations and version hash; ask Daria for the original metadata and transformations.
2. Define each building's verified postcode, use, area, HVAC and operating schedule; keep unverified data explicit. Find appropriate Barcelona building-level annual meter benchmarks and Spanish hourly profiles for each use.
3. Compare sector-derived six-hour patterns against independent typology evidence and generate multiple within-band profiles and annual-demand sensitivities. Keep observations, proxy assumptions and EnergyPlus simulations separately labeled. Do not train on proxies as if they were meter readings.
4. Verify A160 geometry and run EnergyPlus; validate simulations before training a separately versioned surrogate. Evaluate against baselines using held-out configurations and report limits.
5. Reconcile Breno's sharing, battery and economic methods with this project's time, provenance and conservation contracts. Use independent aligned demand for evaluation and compare host-only and other baselines, including no-gain conditions; identify direct-wiring versus grid-sharing assumptions.
6. Add defensible outputs to the interface only after review; retain sourced, hypothetical and pending labels. Team members can independently review the data, physics, allocation and interface, then reconcile assumptions at each gate.
