PROJECT DECISION
- Primary rooftop PV host: A160, Carrer dels Almogavers 160, Barcelona, Spain. Purpose-built office completed 2022 (Batlleiroig). Gross constructed area 13,385 m2; developer reports 9,400 m2 office area; these are NOT conditioned floor areas. Stepped south terraces and greenery are not automatically PV-ready. Actual roof PV capacity, structural suitability, rooftop obstacles, and meter data remain unverified.
- Research objective: compare A160 host-only rooftop PV against hypothetical collective sharing among a small nearby MIXED-USE cluster. Focus environmental impact, economic viability and social allocation. No real-time trading, new PV physical model, private meters, or claims of legal approval.
- Case-study consumers, subject to cadastral geometry, network eligibility and time-series checks:
  1) ITAEB, Institut de Tecniques Audiovisuals i de l'Espectacle de Barcelona, Almogavers 131; EDUCATION. Operating schedules published: 08:00–14:10 morning and 15:00–21:10 afternoon for selected curricula. It moved to this location in September 2025, reusing/adapting the former modular school site. Old 2018 plans are not necessarily 2026 as-built geometry. No public whole-building hourly electricity meter.
  2) Municipal social-rental housing, Carrer de la Ciutat de Granada 95–97; RESIDENTIAL. Opened/delivered May 2024. 18 occupied homes: 10 approximately 45 m2 and 8 approximately 55 m2; municipal architectural/contract documents exist, and the scheme has heat-pump/aerothermal design. A 2021 municipal description says rooftop PV was planned for common services: VERIFY CURRENT INSTALLATION before counting net electricity demand or PV. No public dwelling-level/hourly meters; never infer resident vulnerabilities or individual lifestyles.
  3) NH Barcelona Diagonal Poblenou (formerly NH Barcelona Diagonal Center), Carrer d'Alaba 94–96; HOSPITALITY. Hotel website confirms 129 rooms on 6 floors, cooling, gym and event facilities. Continuous operation and occupancy-dependent load are useful alternative operating scenarios. Actual occupancy, energy system specifications, total conditioned floor area and electricity meter are not public/verified.
  4) Optional comparator: Almogavers 165, municipal Care Economy Hub with Hospital del Mar healthcare vocational-training rooms; MIXED PUBLIC/EDUCATION. Documented 20 kWp solar installation and publicly documented use programme; do not treat entire building as ordinary office. Verify whether existing generation serves the building and whether a hypothetical shared case can include it without double counting.
- Secondary non-core office comparator: Badajoz 97; same development, supported by architecture, but don't allow it to crowd out the varied-use case.

WHY THIS CLUSTER
- Four clearly different electricity-use profiles: conventional office (weekday daytime), school (morning and afternoon term times), residential (mornings/evenings/weekends), hospitality (24h and seasonal).
- Public building identity, uses and proxy operation schedules; social rental context offers transparent comparison of renewable access WITHOUT assigning personal socioeconomic attributes.
- Distinct schedules can test whether sharing genuinely increases PV self-consumption compared to A160 host-only. If A160 already absorbs all its generation, no extra local energy benefit may exist; report this honestly.
- Public building-level hourly measured consumption is NOT available. Physics simulations are scenario references, not observed truth.
- Geographic proximity is plausible; DO NOT assert statutory eligibility until checking exact cadastral coordinates, electrical connection and current collective-self-consumption rules.

DEMAND MODEL: KEEP THE SCIENTIFIC CLAIMS SEPARATE
- Active V2 AI is XGBoost trained on EnergyPlus-simulated SMALL US REFERENCE OFFICE (~511.16 m2), Barcelona IWEC reference climate, 36 operation configurations; test MAE around 0.648 kWh per hourly interval against same-simulator test configurations. NOT validated on A160 or measured local electricity.
- Do NOT predict hotel, residential, school or whole A160 by applying this office model or by merely multiplying by floor area.
- For minimal viable research: adapt geometry for A160; simulate representative independent reference demand for hotel, school and residential using established EnergyPlus archetypes and sourced use/schedule assumptions. These are independent physics-based scenarios, and only office demand is estimated by the ML surrogate, unless more training time is allocated.
- Possible extension if feasible: expand a single XGBoost surrogate with building_use, floor_area, shape, envelope/HVAC and operation inputs and TRAINING SIMULATIONS spanning those features. Compare against per-type schedule-aware baselines; split by whole building geometry/configuration, and test unseen weather and operation combinations. Avoid claim of cross-typology generalization without validation.
- Freeze a plan based on forecast inputs, then compare its value on independent EnergyPlus reference load series; do not score predicted-share allocations against the same predicted loads as proof of AI impact.
- Always compare grid-only, A160-only, equal/fixed collective, AI-informed pre-agreed hourly coefficient schemes. Conserve PV and demand, no claimed environmental gain if no actual grid-import reduction.
- Standardize Barcelona civil/UTC time and one coherent weather year; V2 office model's 2017 standard-time + IWEC typical-year conventions are not directly identical to PVGIS UTC / export minute offsets.
- Pre-agreed allocation coefficients are hypothetical and require current legal/settlement review. Do not call them real-time dynamic market transactions.

- The consolidated Spanish RD 244/2019 was updated by RDL 7/2026 (effective March 22, 2026); relevant PV/wind qualifying conditions include a 5,000 m proximity option for up-to-5-MW installations. The earlier 2025 5-km attempt under RDL 7/2025 was repealed in July 2025; never cite it as current legal basis.
- Source current consolidated BOE: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2019-5089
- Amendment: https://boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-6544
- Repeal of earlier 2025 law: https://boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-15313

- Under RD 244/2019, hourly coefficients must be pre-agreed, sum to 1 for each hour, and be notified as required; no real-time AI dispatch. Official FAQ: https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/energia/energia-electrica/electricidad/autoconsumo-electrico/preguntas-frecuentes-autoconsumo.html
